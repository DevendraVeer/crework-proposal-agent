Subject: Proposal for Northbound Coaching — Client Onboarding Automation

## Overview
Northbound Coaching currently onboards each new client manually: a welcome
email, a scheduling link, an intake form, and a reminder sequence, all sent
by hand from a shared inbox. This proposal automates the entire onboarding
sequence so it triggers the moment a new client signs, freeing up hours
per week currently spent on repetitive admin.

## Scope of Work
- Trigger the full onboarding sequence from a single event: a new row in
  the client tracking sheet.
- Automatically send the welcome email, intake form link and first
  scheduling link in the correct order and timing.
- Add reminder logic for clients who haven't completed intake within 48
  hours.
- Give the team visibility into onboarding status for every client without
  needing to check the inbox.

## Deliverables
- Automated onboarding sequence, fully configured for Northbound's current
  five-email flow.
- A simple dashboard view of onboarding status per client.
- Documentation for updating email copy or timing without engineering
  help.

## Timeline
10 business days from kickoff to live system, including one week of
parallel testing alongside the existing manual process before full
cutover.

$1,800 flat for the build. No recurring cost — this is yours to run.
