Subject: Proposal for Arden & Cole Consulting — New Client Welcome Sequence

## Overview
Arden & Cole currently kicks off every new engagement with a handful of
manual steps: a welcome email, a shared drive folder set up by hand, a
kickoff call scheduled over back-and-forth email, and a contract sent
separately. This proposal turns all of that into a single automated
sequence triggered the moment a new engagement is marked "signed."

## Scope of Work
- Trigger the full welcome sequence from one event: a status change in the
  client tracking sheet.
- Auto-create the client's shared drive folder from a standard template.
- Send the welcome email and a scheduling link for the kickoff call in the
  same step.
- Send the contract for signature and track completion status
  automatically.

## Deliverables
- End-to-end automated welcome sequence, from signed status to kickoff
  call booked.
- Standardized drive folder template applied automatically per client.
- Status tracking so nothing falls through the cracks between steps.

## Timeline
8 business days from kickoff to live system.

## Investment
$1,500 flat for the build. No recurring fee.
