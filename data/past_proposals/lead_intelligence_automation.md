Subject: Proposal for Summit Creative — Inbound Lead Response System

## Overview
Summit Creative's inbound leads come primarily through Instagram DMs and a
website contact form, and right now response time depends entirely on
whoever notices the message first. Warm prospects are going cold before
anyone replies. This proposal covers an AI system that drafts a
personalized reply to every new inbound message within minutes, and routes
it to your phone for a one-tap approval before anything is sent.

## Scope of Work
- Connect Instagram DMs and the website contact form into a single intake
  pipeline.
- Build an AI drafting step that reads each message, pulls relevant context
  (service interest, urgency signals) and writes a reply in Summit
  Creative's existing voice.
- Build a mobile approval flow: every draft arrives as a notification with
  Approve, Edit and Skip options.
- Log every lead and its outcome so patterns are visible over time.

## Deliverables
- Working intake-to-draft pipeline covering DMs and web form submissions.
- Mobile approval interface (Telegram-based).
- A short internal doc explaining how to adjust the tone/template later.

## Timeline
2 weeks from kickoff to live pilot, with the first week focused on the
drafting quality and the second on the approval flow and testing against
real inbound volume.

## Investment
$2,400 flat for the build, no ongoing subscription — you own the system.
Optional $300/month for monitoring and monthly tuning of the response
templates.
